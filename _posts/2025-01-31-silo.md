I've mentioned before that at the beginning of the pandemic, I effectively had to stop shooting photos and took up drawing instead. I'm excited that at the moment everyone is finally getting into Silo now that there's an AppleTV show! I was reading the books back in 2021 and it inspired me to make this series of abstract drawings based on architecture photos that I found interesting.
